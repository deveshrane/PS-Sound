# PS-Sound

Vibrations and resonance — ICSE Class X Physics, Chapter 7 (Sound), section B.
Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve the folder). Eight simulations on one page:

| Topic | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Natural frequency | Pendulum, spring, stretched string and air column, each at the frequency its formula gives |
| Damped | A pendulum in vacuum, air, water or oil: live trace, energy turning into heat |
| String & sonometer | Standing waves; pluck the wire anywhere; fork and paper rider |
| Organ pipes | Closed (1 : 3 : 5) and open (1 : 2 : 3) air columns |
| Forced & resonance | Driven body, build-up, and a measured resonance curve |
| Barton's & forks | Experiments 2 and 1: pendulums on a rubber string, forks on sound boxes |
| Resonance tube | Experiment 3 with readings table; the test-tube question |
| Around us | Soldiers on a bridge, rattling vehicle, radio tuning, singer and glass |

Press **Sound** to hear the tones (strings, pipes, forks, radio, glass). Links like `index.html#tube` open a topic directly.
